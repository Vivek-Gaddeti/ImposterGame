📖 How to Play
Simple rules, sneaky fun!
1
Add players and pick your settings.
2
Each player gets a word. Most get the same word — but 1+ Impostors get a different word!
3
Give clues about your word. Impostors must blend in by listening to others!
4
Vote! Everyone votes on who they think is the Impostor. Most votes = ejected!
5
Game Continues until all Impostors are caught, OR the Impostors outnumber the Crewmates.
